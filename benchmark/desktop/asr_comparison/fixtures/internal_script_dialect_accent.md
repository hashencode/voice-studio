# Consented dialect and accent recording script

Use separate speakers and sessions for development and held-out roles. Record a
self-identified Sichuan or Chongqing variety and at least one independently
reviewed non-target regional accent. Do not infer a dialect from a locale code.

Suggested prompts:

- 我们今天下午三点半讨论项目进度，预算大约是十二万五千元。
- 请把会议纪要发给研发、测试和客户成功团队。
- 明天成都可能下雨，出门前记得查看交通情况。
- 这个方案的准确率提高了百分之十五，但延迟不能增加。
- 新版本将在六月三十日发布，回滚窗口保留四十八小时。

Record consent, the speaker’s own variety label, microphone class, room
condition, and session-role assignment outside Git. An independent reviewer
must confirm the reference and variety label before freezing hashes.
